Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 July 2026

1. Introduction

Chicken Brûlée ("the Service", "we", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your data when you use the Service. The Service is operated in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) (EU) 2016/679 and the laws of Malta.

2. Data Controller

The data controller for the Service is the Service administrator, reachable through the Service's hosting platform. For GDPR purposes, the Service provider acts as both a data controller (for account data) and a data processor (for Discord message data scanned at your direction).

3. Data We Collect

Account data: When you create an account, we collect your email address and a hashed password. We use Supabase Auth for authentication; Supabase's privacy policy also applies to authentication data.

Bot configuration data: You provide a Discord bot token and channel IDs. These are stored encrypted at rest and are never shared, sold, or exposed to third parties.

Scanned Discord data: When you trigger a scan, the Service reads publicly visible messages from the Discord channels you configure. We store: message timestamps, speaker usernames, message content (excerpts), channel IDs, and our automated classification results (themes, severity, signals). This data is stored solely to power the analytics dashboard you see.

4. Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect:

  • Discord private messages or direct messages.
  • Discord user emails, IP addresses, or personal identifiers beyond publicly visible usernames.
  • Data from channels you have not explicitly configured for scanning.
  • Any data from servers where your bot has not been invited.
  • Cookies, tracking pixels, analytics scripts, or any form of user tracking.

5. How We Use Your Data

All collected data is used exclusively to:

  • Display the analytics dashboard to your account.
  • Generate automated summaries and insights (when AI features are enabled).
  • Improve the Service's classification algorithms (in aggregate, anonymised form).

We do not sell, rent, share, or monetise your data. We do not use your data for advertising or marketing.

6. Data Storage and Security

Data is stored in a Supabase PostgreSQL database with the following security measures:

  • Row-Level Security (RLS): Each database row is access-controlled so that your account can only read your own data. Other users cannot access your data under any circumstances.
  • Encryption at rest: Bot tokens are stored encrypted. All database connections use TLS.
  • Encryption in transit: All communication between your browser, the Service, Supabase, and the Discord API uses HTTPS/TLS.
  • Read-only bot permissions: The bot only requires Read Messages and Read Message History permissions. It cannot send messages, modify channels, or access any administrative functions.

7. Data Retention

Your scanned Discord data is retained until you delete your account or request deletion. When you delete your account, all associated data (profile, bot configuration, scans, and observations) is permanently deleted from the database. There is no backup retention or secondary storage.

8. Your Rights (GDPR)

Under GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access: You may request a copy of all data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification: You may correct inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure: You may delete your account and all associated data at any time.
  • Right to restrict processing: You may request that we stop processing your data.
  • Right to data portability: You may request your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Right to object: You may object to the processing of your data.

To exercise any of these rights, contact the Service administrator. We will respond within 30 days as required by GDPR.

9. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data on the following legal bases:

  • Consent: You provide explicit consent by creating an account and configuring the Service to scan your Discord channels.
  • Legitimate interest: Processing is necessary to provide the analytics service you have requested.

You may withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account.

10. International Data Transfers

Data is stored on Supabase servers. Depending on your Supabase project region, data may be stored in the EU, US, or Asia-Pacific. By default, data is stored in the region you select when creating your Supabase project. For users in the European Economic Area, we recommend selecting an EU-based Supabase region.

11. Third-Party Services

The Service relies on the following third-party services:

  • Supabase: Authentication and database hosting. Subject to Supabase's privacy policy.
  • Discord API: Message retrieval. Subject to Discord's privacy policy and developer terms.
  • AI Classification Service (optional): AI-powered classification and summarisation. Only used if you configure an API key. Message excerpts are transmitted for processing but not stored by the service.

12. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children. Discord's own Terms of Service require users to be at least 13 years old (or the digital age of consent in their country).

13. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated where reasonably possible. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

14. Contact and Complaints

For privacy-related inquiries or to exercise your GDPR rights, contact the Service administrator. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Office of the Information and Data Protection Commissioner of Malta (idpc.org.mt) or your local data protection authority.
