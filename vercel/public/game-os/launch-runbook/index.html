LaunchLaunch Runbook

Everything that has to happen before and after you press Release on Steam, with a date on each step. Set your launch day and this works back from it: Steam’s review and Coming Soon deadlines, price and launch discount, press keys, launch day and the first 30 days.

Your launch

Where the dates come from

  • Steam rule tasks come from Steamworks documentation, checked . Each links to its page. Valve changes these rules from time to time, so follow the link when a date matters.
  • Suggested tasks are common practice, not requirements. Skip the ones that don’t fit your plan.
  • Business days skip weekends but not public holidays. Dates that would land on a weekend move to the Friday before.
  • Your ticks and own tasks are saved to your account, separately for each game. Other people on the team keep their own list.
