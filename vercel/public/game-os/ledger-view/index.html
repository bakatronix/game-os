How the Momentum Score works

  • Each figure a tool sends to your game is scored from 0 to 100 against the benchmark listed next to it. Figures with no benchmark stay in the ledger but don’t move the score.
  • Scores roll up into four pillars: Market, Store page, Players and Steam traction. Where two figures measure the same thing (say, positive reviews from the PMF Analyzer and from the daily Steam snapshot), only the newest counts.
  • Pillars are weighted by your game’s stage, set in the bar at the top: market evidence counts most at Concept, Steam traction most after launch. Pillars with no data drop out and the others are rescaled; coverage shows how much of the full picture you have.
  • The history line recalculates the score at each point in time from what was known then.
  • For games with a Steam App ID, Game OS records Steam reviews, recent reviews and players every day at 06:00 UTC. Use Record now to take one immediately.
