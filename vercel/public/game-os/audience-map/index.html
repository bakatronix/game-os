Where do your players live? Start from games like yours and see where their players gather, which other games they play, the words they use, and whether your game’s name is free.

Games like yours

Up to eight comps. They come from your game’s thesis when it has a comp set; add or remove any here.

    Name check

    Is your game’s name free? Checks Steam for games with the same name and whether the .com is registered. Social handles aren’t checked: look those up yourself.

    Where the data comes from

    • Communities: the links each comp lists on its Steam store page. Discord member and online counts are live from Discord’s public invite lookup. Reddit doesn’t allow automated reads, so subreddits are linked without member counts.
    • Neighbouring games: Steam’s “more like this” list for each comp. A game recommended alongside several of your comps shares their audience.
    • Player tags: the tags Steam players voted onto your comps, weighted by votes. Grey tags are generic ones most games carry.
    • Discussion topics: words and two-word phrases from the newest threads in each comp’s Steam discussion forum, counted once per thread. They show what players raise, not polished marketing copy.
