    How scoring works. 1 = 1 point, 2 = 2, 3 = 3, HR = 4. A team's total is the sum of every scorer's points for it, so a team's total keeps growing as more people score it — check Scored by to see how many have. The running total adds up the points through the running order. Your scores are saved to your Game OS account; everyone signed in sees the combined totals, refreshed every few seconds.
